# Chapter 4 Physics Model Contract (initial)
Quantitative results must use inputs with declared units; **illustrative material coefficients** never imply device calibration.

1. **Propagation phase** at fixed λ0 and real effective-index change Δneff: Δφ=(2π/λ0)Δneff L. λ0,L same length unit. Phase and |E|² do not mean the same thing. For a symmetric ideal balanced MZI port, T1=cos²(Δφ/2), T2=sin²(Δφ/2); port convention is explicit.
2. **Plasma dispersion:** silicon free-electron/hole effects may alter both Δn and Δα; measured complex-index dependence, wavelength, doping and free-carrier distribution are needed for a calibrated model. No arbitrary density→optical response curve permitted unless identified explicitly as a numerical *illustrative assumption* and not a physical fit.
3. **Abrupt PN depletion:** W=√[(2ε_si/q)(1/NA+1/ND)(Vbi+VR)] for reverse VR≥0 and uniform doping in SI. Vbi=(kBT/q)ln(NAND/ni²) under ideal nondegenerate equilibrium assumptions. This is a 1D electrostatic depletion approximation; no optical overlap, 3D junction, breakdown or TCAD.
4. **RC:** |H(f)|/|H(0)|=1/√[1+(2πfRC)²], f3dB=1/(2πRC) for lumped linear first-order RC. Not an RF traveling-wave or 200G EO bandwidth prediction.
5. **VπL:** Vπ defined only from an explicitly assumed linear voltage-to-Δneff sensitivity. For ideal phase shifter ∂φ/∂V=(2π/λ0)L∂neff/∂V; VπL=λ0/(2|∂neff/∂V|) if sensitivity constant and nonzero. No fake carrier→sensitivity calibration.
6. **Ring:** Can reuse established Chapter 2 all-pass transfer with round-trip phase θ, self-coupling r and single-pass amplitude a, keeping passivity 0–1. Ideal resonance shift Δλ≈(λ0/ng)Δneff for small changes and fixed geometry/coupling, with explicit ng. Optical cavity photon lifetime and EO RC are distinct; avoid inferring electro-optic bandwidth from one static plot.
7. **PIN attenuation:** T=exp(−Δα L), where Δα is a directly given nonnegative *power* attenuation coefficient in 1/length. If α defined as field-amplitude attenuation, exponent convention differs. Carrier density→α is outside uncalibrated analytical model.
8. **Thermal tuning:** Δneff=(dneff/dT)ΔT and Δφ=(2π/λ0)Δneff L, with given effective thermo-optic sensitivity and no claim of silicon bulk thermo-optic coefficient as full waveguide value. First-order T(t)=Tamb+Rth P[1−exp(−t/τ)] for applied heater power step in a lumped model; τ=Rth Cth. Thermal phase and dynamic optical power must evaluate the same state.
9. Distinguish Illustrative geometry drawings, Analytical declared calculations, Numerical validated models, and measured data. No numerical/full-vector device model included in M0.
10. Unit tests required: PN VR monotonic for valid domains, MZI complement, Δφ proportionality, RC f3dB, ring passivity/periodicity and resonance shift sign, attenuation bounds, heat step limits, unit conversions, saved bounds and legacy regression.
